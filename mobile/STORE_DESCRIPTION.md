# Peacee1 Money

## Short description

Track income, expenses and savings goals for yourself and your family.

## Full description

Build better money habits with Peacee1 Money, a simple app for tracking everyday income and expenses.

Keep your personal records organized or manage a shared family ledger. Your records sync with Peacee1 Finance on the web, so you can access the same account from your phone or computer.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

• Record income and expenses with categories, dates and notes.
• Edit transactions and organize your own categories with custom colors.
• Review monthly summaries and spending by category.
• Browse your transactions on a calendar.
• Use a shared family ledger and see who recorded each transaction.
• Create savings goals and update your progress.
• Request optional AI spending reviews and save the reviews you want to revisit.
• Track multiple supported currencies with separate totals, without automatic conversion.
• Personalize your experience with color themes, light or dark mode and supported languages.
• Set optional reminders to build a consistent recording habit.

A SHARED PEACEE1 ACCOUNT

Use your Peacee1 account on the app and website. Your financial records are stored on the server for synchronization. Internet access is required for account access and synchronization.

FEATURE CREDITS

Some features, including AI reviews and adding or editing custom categories, use in-app reward coins. These coins are feature credits and cannot be redeemed for cash.

Peacee1 Money is a financial record-keeping tool. It does not automatically import personal bank transactions or hold your money. AI reviews are informational and may contain mistakes; they are not professional financial advice.
